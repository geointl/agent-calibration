Triaxial Test Report - Sample TX-001

Test Type: Consolidated undrained (CU) Triaxial Compression

Sample Preparation:
- Soil type: Loose sand
- Initial void ratio: approximately 0.760
- Relative density: Loose

Test Conditions:
- Consolidation pressure: 100 kPa (isotropic)
- Drainage during shear: undrained
- Loading rate: Strain controlled at 0.1%/min

The sample was sheared to approximately 22% axial strain.
Measurements include deviatoric stress (q), mean effective stress (p),
and excess pore water pressure (ue).
